About the Lab Equipment Portal

A single place for students to discover laboratory equipment at IIT Roorkee, see when laboratories are accessible and book available units.

How the information is maintained

Equipment records were imported from the laboratory inventory lists supplied by each lab and are now maintained by laboratory staff in the Staff Portal. Each record keeps a reference to its original source line.

  • Quantities are taken directly from the inventories.
  • Condition is recorded per unit — working, partially functional, damaged — and units stay Not inspected until staff record them. The source lists did not include condition.
  • Room numbers and timetables appear once staff enter verified values; the portal never guesses them.
  • Images are representative illustrations of the equipment type unless marked as a photo.

Booking rules

  • Only students with a verified IIT Roorkee institutional email can book.
  • Damaged units are never bookable; items restricted to research use or withdrawn by staff cannot be booked at all.
  • Bookings run {{ config.BOOKING_DAY_START }}–{{ config.BOOKING_DAY_END }}, {{ config.BOOKING_MIN_MINUTES }} minutes to {{ config.BOOKING_MAX_HOURS }} hours, up to {{ config.BOOKING_MAX_DAYS_AHEAD }} days ahead, and never overlap a Class or Reserved period of the laboratory.
  • Requests covered by inspected working units are confirmed immediately; requests that rely on uninspected units stay Pending until staff confirm.
  • You can cancel any booking until it ends.
